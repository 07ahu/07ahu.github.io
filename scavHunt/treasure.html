We hope you had an awesome time exploring drains around Beaufort! It's important to recognize, though, that this scavenger hunt isn't just about solving riddles. It's also about giving you a greater understanding of our waterways. When pieces of trash end up in these drains, they can travel to our bodies of water, like Taylor's Creek, which you probably saw on your voyage. By completing the scavenger hunt and allowing yourself to learn more about water quality and storm drains, you are helping to protect the health, safety, beauty, and future of our community and environment. We want to thank you for taking part in this adventure and getting involved in our community.
